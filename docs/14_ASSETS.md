# 14 — Assets

## Visual contract
Сохраняем текущий approved RPChess UI, фоны, board skins, портреты, фигуры, environment assets и VFX, если они подходят Reboot.

### Standard for new living chess pieces
- PNG, 1024×1024, transparent background.
- Full body, centered, generous transparent margins.
- Strong readable silhouette at small tactical-board scale.
- Consistent front/slight tactical 3/4 camera and lighting.
- No environment, text, logo, watermark or frame.
- Each piece is a separate file, not a sprite sheet.

## Universal base prompt
> Create a premium stylized dark-fantasy living chess warrior for RPChess. Full-body character, centered, isolated on a transparent background, strong readable silhouette, designed to remain recognizable at small tactical-board scale. The character must visually communicate the role of a classical chess piece through silhouette, armor, headgear and equipment, while remaining a believable living fantasy character. Painterly stylized fantasy, semi-illustrative, elegant proportions, detailed but not photorealistic, clear front / slight tactical 3/4 presentation, consistent lighting, no environment, no text, no logo, no watermark, no frame. 1024×1024 PNG with generous transparent margins.

Append one of the following role/race prompts.

## Variant naming
Future variants use predictable names such as `undead_pawn_01.png`, `undead_pawn_02.png`, `elf_knight_01.png`, etc. Character identity can reference a reusable visual archetype so hundreds of named recruits do not require hundreds of fully unique base models.
